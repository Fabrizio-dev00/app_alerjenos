## English (en-US)

**Name (max 30):** SanoScan: Allergen Label Scanner

**Short description (max 80):** Check food labels in 6 languages for your allergens. No account, no ads.

**Full description:**

Living with food allergies, celiac disease, or caring for someone who does? SanoScan helps you check an ingredient list in seconds, even when the label is in another language.

📷 Snap the ingredient list
SanoScan reads the text, detects the language, translates it and looks for the allergens in your profile.

🔎 Clear results
• Contains: the allergen is in the ingredients.
• May contain traces: the maker warns about possible cross-contamination.
• Not detected: we did not find your allergens in the text we read.

SanoScan never says "safe". We show the exact word we found, original and translated, and highlight it on the photo so you can check.

🌍 6 offline languages
Spanish, English, French, German, Italian and Portuguese. Download your languages before you travel and scan without internet.

🗣️ Tell Sano your allergens
Say them out loud or type them. Voice is processed on your phone.

🔒 Real privacy
No account, no sign-up, no ads. Your profiles, history and photos stay on your phone.

Allergens: gluten (wheat, barley, rye, oats), milk, lactose, egg, peanuts, tree nuts, soy, fish, shellfish (crustaceans and molluscs), sesame, mustard, celery, sulphites and lupin.

⭐ SanoScan Pro (one-time purchase, no subscription): unlimited scans, up to 6 family profiles and history that is never deleted.

⚠️ Important: SanoScan is an aid, not a medical device. Text recognition can fail. Always check the physical label and ask your doctor if in doubt.

**ASO keywords:** allergen scanner, food allergy, celiac, gluten free, lactose intolerance, label scanner, ingredients checker, translate food labels, travel with allergies, peanut allergy, milk allergy.
